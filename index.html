I am a Research Engineer in the ANTIQUE team at ENS-PSL / Inria / CNRS, working under Prof. Caterina Urban. My work focuses on neural specifications for formal methods in explainable AI.

My research interests lie in theoretical machine learning, explainable AI, and optimization. Previously, I worked on simplifying post-trained ReLU neural networks for mixed-integer linear programming systems at OVGU, and on data-driven decision analysis for energy systems at EPFL.

I graduated from École Polytechnique with an MSc&T in Artificial Intelligence & Advanced Visual Computing (AiViC, MX23).
